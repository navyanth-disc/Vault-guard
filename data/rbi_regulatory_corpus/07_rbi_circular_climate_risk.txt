RESERVE BANK OF INDIA
CIRCULAR ON CLIMATE RISK MANAGEMENT AND SUSTAINABLE FINANCING FOR COMMERCIAL BANKS
1. Preamble: Commercial banks shall incorporate mandatory Climate Risk Disclosure norms in their credit underwriting processes.
2. Green Lending Thresholds: Exposure to renewable energy projects up to INR 30 Crore shall qualify for priority underwriting.
3. Reporting Norms: Banks shall report carbon intensity metrics for enterprise borrowers annually.
